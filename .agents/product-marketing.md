## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Rihla Reach connects quality halal restaurants with authentic influencer voices. We verify, vet, and measure, because both creators and restaurants deserve partnerships that matter.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Halal restaurants struggle to reach new halal-conscious diners in a way they can trust and measure.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- General influencer agencies don't understand halal standards or the audience, so they risk poor-fit or culturally tone-deaf partnerships [INFERRED]
- Doing it yourself means finding creators by hand, vetting fake followers, negotiating, and chasing content
- Paid social and delivery-app ads buy impressions, not trust [INFERRED]
