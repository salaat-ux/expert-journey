## 1. Brand Concept

### Mission
Connect authentic halal dining experiences with influential voices, amplifying quality restaurants through trusted creator partnerships.

### Core Values
- **Authenticity**: Genuine halal certification and transparency
- **Quality**: Curated partnerships, not transactional deals
- **Expertise**: Deep knowledge of halal standards and creator markets
- **Respect**: Cultural sensitivity in all communications
- **Impact**: Measurable results for both influencers and restaurants

### Target Audiences

**Influencers (Content Creators)**
- Micro/macro influencers in food, lifestyle, travel niches
- Audience: Halal-conscious, UK-based or UK-interested
- Motivation: Authentic partnership opportunities, fair compensation

**Restaurant Owners & Managers**
- Halal restaurants seeking to expand reach
- Motivation: Qualified traffic, verified halal audience, professional campaigns

**End Consumers**
- Halal-conscious diners, Muslim communities
- Motivation: Discovering new halal restaurants, trusted recommendations

## 3. Typography

### Font Stack

```css
--font-heading: 'Poppins', 'Segoe UI', sans-serif;
--font-body: 'Lora', 'Georgia', serif;
--font-mono: 'Menlo', 'Monaco', monospace;
```

**Why Poppins + Lora?**
- **Poppins**: Modern, geometric headings signal professionalism and approachability
- **Lora**: Elegant, highly readable serif body text conveys authority and quality

### Type Scale

| Element | Font | Weight | Desktop | Mobile | Line Height | Usage |
|---------|------|--------|---------|--------|-------------|-------|
| H1 (Hero) | Poppins | 700 | 48px | 32px | 1.2 | Page titles, brand statements |
| H2 (Section) | Poppins | 600 | 36px | 28px | 1.25 | Major sections, key messages |
| H3 (Subsection) | Poppins | 600 | 28px | 24px | 1.3 | Topic headers, grouping |
| H4 (Small Header) | Poppins | 600 | 24px | 20px | 1.35 | Card titles, list headers |
| Body | Lora | 400 | 16px | 16px | 1.6 | Main content, descriptions |
| Small | Lora | 400 | 14px | 14px | 1.5 | Secondary info, metadata |
| Caption | Lora | 400 | 12px | 12px | 1.4 | Image captions, fine print |
| Label | Poppins | 500 | 12px | 12px | 1.4 | Form labels, badges |
| Button | Poppins | 600 | 14px | 14px | 1.4 | Call-to-action text |

### Font Pairing Rules
- Always pair Poppins headings with Lora body
- Never use Poppins for body text (reduced readability)
- Use weight 600+ for Poppins headings for impact
- Use weight 400 for Lora body, 500 for labels

### Clear Space
- Minimum clear space around logo = 1/3 of logo height
- Never place text or graphics within clear space

### Placement Rules
- On Forest Green backgrounds: Use white or light gold
- On light backgrounds: Use Forest Green or full color
- Never reverse colors without approval
- Minimum contrast ratio: 3:1

### Don'ts
- ❌ Don't rotate, skew, or distort
- ❌ Don't change colors outside approved palette
- ❌ Don't add effects (shadows, gradients, glows)
- ❌ Don't crop or modify proportions
- ❌ Don't place on photographic backgrounds without contrast treatment
- ❌ Don't scale below minimum sizes

## 5. Voice & Tone

### Brand Personality

| Trait | Definition |
|-------|-----------|
| **Authentic** | Genuine, transparent, honest. We don't oversell or misrepresent. Halal authenticity is non-negotiable. |
| **Expert** | Knowledgeable, credible, authoritative. We understand both influencer marketing and halal markets deeply. |
| **Inclusive** | Respectful, welcoming, culturally aware. We honor Islamic traditions while speaking to diverse audiences. |

### Voice Chart: How We Communicate

| Aspect | We Are | We Are Not |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| **Formality** | Professional and respectful | Casual or flippant |
| **Energy** | Confident and purposeful | Hype-driven or sensational |
| **Expertise** | Knowledgeable and evidence-based | Pretentious or gatekeeping |
| **Tone** | Warm but authoritative | Cold or overly familiar |
| **Cultural Respect** | Celebratory of halal traditions | Tokenizing or surface-level |

### Tone by Context

| Context | Tone | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| **Marketing Copy** | Confident, aspirational, quality-focused | "Amplify authentic halal experiences with creators who genuinely inspire." |
| **Email Outreach** | Professional, personalized, respectful | "We've identified your audience alignment with [Restaurant]. Here's why this partnership matters." |
| **Error Messages** | Clear, helpful, apologetic | "We couldn't complete that request. Here's what went wrong and how to fix it." |
| **Success Messages** | Celebratory, specific, forward-looking | "Campaign live! Your first 50 impressions in 2 hours. Track performance here." |
| **Support/FAQ** | Knowledgeable, patient, solution-focused | "Halal certification verification: Here's exactly how we confirm authenticity." |

### Prohibited Terms

| Term | Reason | Use Instead |
|------|--------|-------------|
| "Exotic halal" | Orientalizing, implies otherness | "Authentic halal" |
| "Trendy halal" | Trivializes religious practice | "Growing halal market" |
| "Halal influencers" (as category) | Stereotyping, too narrow | "Influencers in the halal space" |
| "Targeting Muslims" | Cold, commercial framing | "Reaching halal-conscious audiences" |
| "Cheap deals" | Undermines quality positioning | "Exclusive partnership opportunities" |
| "Viral potential" | Mercenary, unprofessional | "Audience engagement potential" |

### Key Messages (For All Audiences)

**Core Message:**
"Rihla Reach connects quality halal restaurants with authentic influencer voices. We verify, vet, and measure—because both creators and restaurants deserve partnerships that matter."

**For Influencers:**
- Fair compensation based on true reach
- Authentic brands you can genuinely recommend
- Professional campaign management
- Performance transparency

**For Restaurant Owners:**
- Access to verified, engaged halal-conscious audiences
- Influencers whose values align with your brand
- Measurable results and ROI tracking
- Professional campaign execution

**For Consumers (Indirect):**
- Discover new halal restaurants from trusted voices
- Support businesses that prioritize quality
- Connect with a community of conscious diners

## 6. Imagery Guidelines

### Photography Style

**Setting:** Restaurant environments, authentic moments
- **Lighting:** Natural light preferred. Warm, golden-hour photography creates premium feel
- **Subjects:** Real food, real people, genuine reactions. No staged or overly filtered content
- **Composition:** Close-ups of dishes, moments of sharing, community gathering. Avoid empty restaurant shots
- **Color Treatment:** Slightly warm white balance to complement brand colors. Respect food colors—no aggressive filtering

### Illustrations & Graphics

- **Style:** Clean, modern line work or flat design. No cartoonish or overly playful illustrations
- **Colors:** Restricted to brand palette (Forest Green, Emerald, Golden Amber, Neutral palette)
- **Stroke:** 2-3px for consistency
- **Icon Style:** Minimalist outlined style, 24px base grid

### Visual Hierarchy

- Photographs for emotional connection (restaurant ambiance, food appeal)
- Illustrations for process/concept explanation (how partnerships work, onboarding steps)
- Icons for navigation and quick reference (filter by cuisine, verify status, payment indicators)

### Content Guidelines

| Content Type | Style | Tone | Usage |
|---------------|-------|------|-------|
| Campaign Success Stories | Professional photo + quote | Authentic, celebratory | Case studies, testimonials |
| Ingredient/Dish Close-ups | High-quality, appetizing photography | Premium, inviting | Marketing materials |
| Process Diagrams | Clean illustrations, brand colors | Instructional, clear | How-it-works sections |
| Team/People Photos | Professional, authentic, diverse | Approachable, expert | About pages, testimonials |
| Social Media Graphics | Bold typography + imagery | Engaging, professional | Posts, stories, announcements |

## 8. Brand Applications

### Website
- Forest Green navigation, headers, primary CTAs
- Golden Amber for quality signals, success metrics, testimonial accents
- Lora body text for readability, Poppins headings for hierarchy
- Generous white space, premium feel without clutter

### Social Media
- Consistent use of Forest Green and Emerald in graphics
- Golden Amber highlights for important announcements
- Mix of photography (restaurant, food) and illustrated graphics
- Professional, high-quality visual content only

### Email
- Forest Green button CTAs
- Lora body text for long-form content
- Poppins headlines for scannability
- Clean, spacious layouts
- Always include halal authentication badge/seal where relevant

### Presentations
- Forest Green for slide backgrounds or accents
- Poppins for all headers and bullet points
- Golden Amber for highlights/emphasis
- High-quality food photography or illustrations
- Consistent page numbering and footer branding

### Marketing Collateral
- Business cards: Forest Green + white, Poppins for company name, Lora for contact info
- Letterhead: Forest Green brand mark, muted neutral palette
- Proposals: Professional, branded cover page, consistent headers throughout

## 9. Brand Consistency Checklist

Before launching any content, verify:

- [ ] **Color Accuracy**: Colors match approved hex codes (within 2% tolerance)
- [ ] **Typography**: Headings use Poppins, body uses Lora
- [ ] **Tone**: Content aligns with Voice Chart (authentic, expert, inclusive)
- [ ] **Messaging**: References brand pillars or key messages
- [ ] **Imagery**: Photography follows style guidelines; no overly filtered content
- [ ] **Accessibility**: Text/background contrast meets WCAG 2.1 AA minimum
- [ ] **Logo Usage**: Logo placement, sizing, and spacing follow guidelines (if applicable)
- [ ] **Prohibited Terms**: No excluded language used
- [ ] **Audience Relevance**: Message speaks clearly to intended audience
- [ ] **Cultural Respect**: Content honors Islamic traditions and halal authenticity
