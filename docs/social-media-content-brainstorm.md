**Date:** August 28, 2026
**Grounded in:** `docs/brand-guidelines.md` (v1.0, July 17 2026)
**Status:** Pre-launch — no logo, no published case studies, no live campaigns yet. Content plan below is built around that reality, not around metrics we don't have yet.

---

## 1. Reality check before brainstorming

Two facts from the brand doc shape everything below:

- **Logo design is still "Phase 1 deliverable"** — no finished brand mark exists yet. Avoid posting anything that presents Rihla Reach as fully live/branded until that's resolved.
- **No case studies, campaign results, or restaurant/influencer roster are documented anywhere in this repo.** The brand doc's example messages ("Campaign live! Your first 50 impressions in 2 hours") are *tone examples*, not real results. Posting them as if they're real testimonials would violate the brand's own "Authentic" value and the prohibited-terms list (no overselling, no misrepresenting).

**Implication:** early content should be *building-in-public / thought-leadership / audience-building*, not proof-of-results. Save results-driven posts for after the first real campaigns exist.

## 3. Concrete post ideas by audience

### For influencers (recruitment/retention)
1. **"What fair compensation actually looks like"** carousel — breaks down how payment-by-true-reach works vs. flat-fee scams. Ties to brand line: *"Get paid fairly for your influence. No scams, no surprises."*
2. **Red-flag/green-flag carousel**: signs of a sketchy brand deal vs. a legitimate one. Positions Rihla as the "green flag" option without naming competitors.
3. **"Work with restaurants you'd actually visit"** — short video explaining the vetting bar for restaurants before they're allowed to partner.
4. Explainer Reel: "How we measure engagement, not just impressions."

### For restaurant owners
1. **"Halal certification ≠ marketing certification"** — explainer on why great food + real certification still isn't enough for discovery, and where influencer marketing fits.
2. **Vetting-criteria post**: "Not every restaurant deserves amplification. Here's our bar." (direct brand pillar line) — turns exclusivity into trust signal.
3. **Myth-busting carousel** on influencer marketing for restaurants (e.g. "you don't need a huge follower count to run an effective campaign" — micro-influencers often outperform macro on engagement; see fact-check below).
4. Founder-voice post: why this company exists — the gap between halal-conscious diners and restaurants that can't get discovered.

### For consumers (indirect/community-building)
1. **"Halal 101" explainer series** — certification standards, what "halal-conscious" audience means, common misconceptions.
2. **Community polls**: "What makes you trust a halal label?" / "Biggest halal dining frustration?"
3. **UGC callouts**: "Tag your favorite halal spot" — seeds a future restaurant pipeline and audience data, no fabricated claims needed.
4. Golden-hour food photography (per imagery guidelines) once real restaurant partners exist — hold until then.

### Shared/brand-building (any audience)
1. **Building-in-public series**: logo-in-progress, brand system decisions, "why we chose forest green" — turns the pre-launch stage into content instead of hiding it.
2. **Market-education posts** using the verified stats in Section 5 below (with sources cited in-caption or in a pinned comment — required per brand's "evidence-based, not pretentious" tone rule).
3. **Prohibited-terms-as-content**: a "words we won't use and why" post (e.g. "exotic halal," "targeting Muslims") — turns the brand's own style guide into a credibility-building, culturally-respectful piece of content.

## 4. Format & platform suggestions

| Platform | Fit | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Instagram** | Primary — carousels for explainers, Reels for founder/process content, Stories for polls | Matches "photography + illustrated graphics" guideline directly |
| **TikTok** | Strong for myth-busting, red-flag/green-flag, and founder-voice short video | Higher organic reach pre-launch when there's no ad budget yet |
| **LinkedIn** | Restaurant-owner and B2B-influencer-market audience; market-education and founder posts | Good fit for "Expertise & Results" pillar |
| **X/Twitter** | Optional — data-driven micro-posts, quick myth-busts | Lower priority unless the target audience is active there |

**Posting cadence, based on current platform research (see sources below):**
- Instagram: aim for weekday midday–evening windows — Wed 12pm & 6pm, Thu 9am, Tue 1–7pm tend to perform well; evenings (6–11pm) are consistently strong across time zones.
- TikTok: Tue–Thu plus Saturday, with a 6–10pm evening peak.
- These are general benchmarks, not Rihla-specific data — treat as a starting point and re-test once there's real account analytics.

## 5. Fact-check

Claims worth using in captions/education content, verified against current sources — figures vary meaningfully by research firm, so treat as **ranges**, not precise numbers, and cite the source if quoting a figure publicly:

- **UK halal food market size**: estimates range widely by methodology — one set of reports puts it around **$89–98B in 2024/2025**, forecast to reach **~$150–205B by 2030–2034** (~8.6–9% CAGR); a separate, much smaller figure (~$149.6M by 2030) appeared in one press release and looks like an outlier/likely unit inconsistency in that particular report. **Recommendation: don't quote a single headline number without naming the source and date** — the spread between sources is too large to state as settled fact.
  - [IMARC Group — UK Halal Food Market](https://www.imarcgroup.com/uk-halal-food-market)
  - [Research and Markets — UK Halal Food Market](https://www.researchandmarkets.com/report/united-kingdom-halal-food-market)
  - [GlobeNewswire — UK Halal Food Market Analysis 2025](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/10/31/3178441/0/en/UK-Halal-Food-Market-Analysis-Report-2025-A-149-61-Million-Market-by-2030-Driven-by-Rising-Premiumization-and-Diversification-of-Halal-Products.html)
- **UK Muslim population**: **3.87 million** in England & Wales per the 2021 Census (6.5% of the population, up from 4.8% in 2011), concentrated in Birmingham, Bradford, Tower Hamlets, Manchester, Newham. This is a solid, citable figure for audience-sizing content.
  - [Muslim Council of Britain — 2021 Census First Look](https://mcb.org.uk/resources/2021-census-first-look/)
- **Best posting times (2026)**: Instagram — Wed 12pm/6pm, Thu 9am, weekday afternoons/evenings generally strong; TikTok — Tue–Thu and Sat, 6–10pm peak. Based on Sprout Social's 2026 analysis of ~2B engagements across 307K profiles — a large sample, but still a general benchmark, not Rihla-specific.
  - [Sprout Social — Best Times to Post on Instagram](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/best-times-to-post-on-instagram/)
  - [Sprout Social — Best Times to Post on TikTok](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/best-times-to-post-on-tiktok/)
  - [Buffer — Best Time to Post on Instagram 2026](https://buffer.com/resources/when-is-the-best-time-to-post-on-instagram/)

**Not fact-checkable / flagged as unverifiable:** any claim implying Rihla Reach already has live campaigns, a specific number of partner restaurants/influencers, or measured results. None of that exists in this repo or was provided — don't publish it until it's real.

## 6. Recommendations

1. **Sequence content to match company stage**: building-in-public + education now → case studies + results content once first campaigns run. Don't skip ahead.
2. **Pin sourcing** on any stat-driven post (market size, population figures) — protects the "evidence-based, not pretentious" tone and avoids the wildly inconsistent market-size numbers coming back to bite credibility.
3. **Use the brand's own prohibited-terms list as a content source**, not just a filter — it doubles as a culturally-literate, differentiating post idea.
4. **Hold "premium" food photography until real restaurant partners exist** — stock/AI-generated food imagery undercuts the "authentic moments, no staged content" imagery guideline.
5. **Start LinkedIn + Instagram first**; treat TikTok as a fast-follow once there's bandwidth to produce short-form video consistently — better to be excellent on two platforms than thin on four.
